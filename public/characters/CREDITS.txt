3D characters
=============

Source models: "Ultimate Animated Character Pack" by Quaternius (Suit_Female,
Suit_Male), https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatedanimatedcharacter.html
License: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication). Free for commercial use; credit
is appreciated but not required.

Restyled in the ResumeATS palette and rendered in Blender (Cycles, GPU) with
scripts/blender/render-character.py, then encoded with FFmpeg:

  blender -b Suit_Female.blend --python scripts/blender/render-character.py -- \
    --action Victory --palette suit --engine CYCLES --out frames/female-victory --size 640
  ffmpeg -framerate 30 -i frames/female-victory/frame_%04d.png \
    -vf "scale=480:480:flags=lanczos,format=yuva420p" -c:v libvpx-vp9 \
    -pix_fmt yuva420p -crf 34 -b:v 0 -an female-victory.webm

Clips: *-idle.webm, *-victory.webm (VP9 with alpha); stills: *-still.webp, *-cheer.webp.
